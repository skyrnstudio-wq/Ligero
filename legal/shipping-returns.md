## Before you order

- Alcohol-based fragrance cannot ship by air. Island and remote pincodes take the full 7 working days.
- Give a delivery address where someone receives parcels in the day. Couriers attempt three times.

## Returns

- Unopened, sealed bottles: 7 days from delivery, refund minus two-way freight.
- Opened bottles: not returnable. Break the seal only when the nose has decided.
- Damaged in transit: photograph packaging and bottle within 48 hours and write to us. We replace or refund, freight ours.
- Refunds reach the original payment method within [7] working days of the return arriving.
