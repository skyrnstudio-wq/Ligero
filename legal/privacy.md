# Privacy Policy

**Ligero Parfum.** Effective date: [insert].

This policy explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it. It is written to be read.

## 1. What we collect

- What you give us: name, email, phone, address when you order or write to the Concierge. Message contents when you write.
- What your visit generates: order and payment records, correspondence history, and standard server records of the pages served. No advertising profiles. No cross-site tracking. We do not run third-party analytics that identifies you.
- What we never collect: sensitive personal data under the DPDP Act. Do not send health or financial details by email.

## 2. Why we collect it (purpose limitation)

- To sell you perfume: orders, invoices, delivery.
- To answer you: Concierge correspondence, private appointments, private commissions.
- With your consent: The Correspondence, our newsletter. Four letters a year. Nothing else. Consent is a specific opt-in, not a pre-ticked box. Withdrawing consent is one click or one reply.

## 3. The legal basis (DPDP Act, 2023)

We process personal data on two grounds only: your consent, and a legitimate use permitted by the Act's Section 7 (delivery of goods, provision of service, processing payment). Nothing else. We are data fiduciary for data we collect directly. When you pay through a payment gateway, the gateway is its own data fiduciary of the data you give it.

## 4. What we do not do

- No sale or rental of personal data. Ever.
- No advertising pixels, no behavioral retargeting, no data broker enrichment.
- No automated decisions about you, no profiling.

## 5. Who processes data with us

- Our payment gateway (charges, refunds).
- Our courier (delivery, the address is unavoidable).
- Our email service (order confirmations, the Correspondence).

Each processes only what the task needs. We do not sell onward. Current processor list is available on request to the Grievance Officer below.

## 6. How long we keep it

- Invoices and order records: 8 years, as tax law requires.
- Correspondence: up to 3 years after the last exchange.
- Correspondence list: until you unsubscribe, then deleted within 30 days.
- Everything else: deleted or anonymized when the purpose ends.

## 7. Your rights (Sections 11 and 13, DPDP Act)

- Ask what we hold: a summary, on request.
- Correction, completion, updating.
- Erasure: we erase unless retaining is required by law (tax records) or needed to defend legal claims.
- Withdraw consent anytime. Withdrawal ends future use. It does not undo past processing.
- Nominate: you may nominate who should receive your data if you cannot act for yourself.

Write to the Grievance Officer. We acknowledge within 72 hours and resolve within 30 days as the Act requires.

## 8. Children

The website is not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If a child's data reaches us, we delete it.

## 9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is limited to the people who need it to do the work described here. Breach: if a breach is likely to cause harm, we notify affected users and the Data Protection Board without delay, as Section 8(6) requires.

## 10. Changes to this policy

Material changes will be announced on this page and, for Correspondence subscribers, in a letter. The effective date above changes with each revision.
