01 / SOURCING

Raw materials come from four farms. We can name them.

02 / BLENDING

Each blend is cold rested before a single bottle is filled.

Time is our primary solvent. Slow cold-maceration in glass demijohns permits volatility to soften naturally, giving the wood notes their peculiar, tactile weight on human skin.
