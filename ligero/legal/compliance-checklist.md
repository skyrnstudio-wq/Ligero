# India Compliance Checklist (internal)

Working doc for launch. Not legal advice. Counsel reviews the final drafts.

## Statutes that bite for a perfume e-commerce site in India

- **DPDP Act, 2023**: consent notice at collection, purpose limitation, grievance officer, breach notification, children's data. Privacy policy drafted accordingly.
- **Consumer Protection (E-commerce) Rules, 2020**: grievance officer published with name and contact, marketplaces vs inventory model disclosures, country of origin, resolutions in 30 days.
- **Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011**: on-site declarations for prepackaged goods: product name, net quantity in ml, MRP inclusive of all taxes, consumer care details, month/year of manufacture, country of origin. Online sellers must display these on the product page.
- **Drugs and Cosmetics Act / CDSCO**: cosmetics license held by the manufacturer/importer, brand owner obligations, "not for medicinal use" context. The house may hold licenses through its manufacturing partner: confirm whose license covers Ligero.
- **Tax**: GST registration and invoices (8-year record retention matches the privacy policy), HS code 3303 for perfumes, e-invoicing thresholds if applicable.

## Popups, banners, consent surfaces: the decision

- **No popups. Ever.** Newsletter is an inline section (The Correspondence), not a popup. Popups are urgency theater and off-voice.
- **No cookie banner.** We run no tracking cookies, no ad pixels, no third-party analytics. Only strictly necessary session/storage needed to run cart and checkout. Nothing to consent to. If analytics are ever added, a notice page and prior notice go first; the checklist updates then.
- **One inline consent line** under the email field in the Correspondence: "Subscribing shares your email with us, for four letters a year and nothing else. Unsubscribe anytime." Linked from the privacy policy.
- **Age gate:** none required by law for fragrance. We skip it. The privacy policy's children clause covers the DPDP angle.

## Voice note

Legal pages are on brand voice: declarative, short sentences, zero em dashes, zero urgency theater. "The nose must decide before the seal breaks" is the register. Compliance reads better when it sounds like a person wrote it.
