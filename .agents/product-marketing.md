# Ligero Parfum: Product Marketing Context

Loaded by the copywriting, marketing-psychology, and CRO skills before they ask questions. Facts live here. Voice rules live in `ligero/brand-voice/SKILL.md`. Sections and page copy live in `ligero/site-blueprint.md`.

## The offer

- Niche perfume house with five permanent fragrances (the Five): Noctis, AUBE, Marée, Ambre Doux, Blanc. Suite is the line that followed: Fleur, Noir Coco. Objects: the Discovery Set (999), En Route car perfume (499). The house story is restraint: the Five are permanent, new entries must earn their place. Client-confirmed.
- Blended in Grasse. Every blend rests ninety days before a single bottle is filled.
- Materials from named sources: Nile delta papyrus, one Calabrian bergamot grove, Breton kelp cut at low tide, a single tobacco estate in La Rioja, iris pallida aged three years.
- Zero synthetic preservatives.
- Private commissions accepted twice a year.

## What the buyer hires the perfume to do

The buyer wants a signature, not a pleasant smell. They hire each fragrance to state something specific about them without words: Noctis states after-dark, Marée states coastal, Blanc states composed. Frame copy around identity, memory, and time of day. Never around "smells good".

## Audience

- Design-conscious adults, roughly 28 to 55, buying primarily for themselves. Gifting is secondary.
- Allergic to hype. Reads ingredient lists. Pays more for provenance and restraint, not for logos.
- Arrives from editorial mentions, word of mouth, and the Correspondence. Not from deal-hunting channels.

## Objections and the honest answer

- "Too expensive": the price is the materials and the ninety days. There are no discounts, ever, so waiting costs nothing and gains nothing.
- "I have never heard of this house": the maison does not argue. It states the farms, Grasse, and the rest period. Facts persuade quietly.
- "I cannot smell a website": notes are described as weather, time, and place so the reader can imagine the scene. The Concierge exists for questions a page cannot answer.

## Proof points (use these, invent no others)

Named farms and estates. Grasse blending. Ninety-day rest. Zero synthetic preservatives. Twice-yearly commissions. That is the entire proof inventory.

## Pricing

Fixed and premium. Never discounted. No sale, promo, or coupon language exists at Ligero. Numerals without currency symbols in display type ("240" not "€240"). Prices appear on /collection and product pages only, never on Home.

## Conversion goals, one intent per page

- Home: "Explore the Collection".
- Collection: "Open [fragrance]".
- Product: "Add to Case".
- All pages: "Request the Correspondence" as the closing section.
- Concierge: "Arrange a private appointment".

## Psychology: what fits, what never

Fits: structural scarcity (the Five are permanent, Suite exists because it earned its place), authority through named sources, reciprocity through the Correspondence, commitment through the Ritual section (a reader who applies perfume as instructed is invested), peak-end (every page closes with the Correspondence, a small gift).

Never: countdowns, fake urgency, low-stock counters (except true batch facts), review stars, testimonial walls, popups, exit-intent modals, discount codes, chat widgets.

## Traffic context

Cold traffic is the assumption. Copy must work for a reader who has never heard the name and gets no retargeting. No copy depends on a previous touchpoint.

## Open questions: ask, never fabricate

- Sample or discovery-set policy. (Drafted as pending in terms.md section 6a.)
- International shipping scope. (Drafted policy is India-only, surface freight.)
- Returns policy for opened fragrance. (Drafted: not returnable; sealed bottles 7 days.)
- Price points per bottle and format sizes.
- Batch sizes and restock cadence.

Legal and compliance drafts live in `ligero/legal/`: privacy policy (DPDP Act 2023), terms of sale, shipping and returns, and the internal compliance checklist. The house runs no ad pixels, no third-party analytics, and no cookie banner; the privacy commitments depend on that staying true.

Any skill needing one of these answers asks the user. It does not invent a number.
